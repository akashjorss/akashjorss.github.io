Privacy policy

rclone-ruche-backup is a personal backup tool. Its only user is its owner, Akash Malhotra.

The tool reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive so that it can copy backups there and check them. The data goes directly between the owner's computers and Google Drive. No data goes to any other server, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Access tokens stay in the rclone configuration on the owner's computers. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
